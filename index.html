Our Story

Three generations of
craftsmanship & trust

Founded in 1989, OM Jewellers began as a small family workshop with one belief — that jewellery should be as honest in its making as it is beautiful in its finish. Today, that belief still guides every piece that leaves our showroom: hallmarked gold, certified diamonds, and designs shaped by hand.

Every purchase comes with transparent purity certification, fair making charges and a buy-back & exchange policy — because we're not just selling jewellery, we're building relationships that last as long as the gold itself.
